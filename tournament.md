# Tournament — food-picture-01 round-3 improvement entry selection

Context: the round-2 incumbent ("Tonkotsu ramen, yolk breaking", a static
yolk-break shot) fell to rank 110/2906, outside the 100 paid places. The
live leaderboard's entire top-12 is one shape — a single bowl of tonkotsu
ramen with a TALL GLOSSY NOODLE PULL, molten/dripping egg, steam, dark
table/background — so all 12 candidates in this round are executions of
that shape, varying pull height/framing, egg placement, steam, oil swirl,
chashu char and crop. No grids, no collages, no text, no multi-dish spreads.

Judged pairwise against the competition's sealed judge rubric, in order:

1. Makes you hungry (instant crave)
2. Food fresh/real/well made — texture: steam, gloss, crunch or melt; not plastic or muddy
3. Styling, light, plating, composition lead the eye to the food
4. Clean craft, no artefacts, garbled text or broken food/hands/tableware
5. Still reads delicious at ~300px wide, main dish clear

## Candidates

- c01 — Tall pull classic: whole bowl, chashu fan, egg dripping to the table
- c02 — Extreme close pull, huge foreground egg (portrait)
- c03 — Wider chili-oil swirl, heavy steam (landscape)
- c04 — Diagonal pull, chashu-heavy (landscape)
- c05 — Low angle, tallest pull, rolled chashu, rim drip
- c06 — Macro strands, nori + scallion, egg bottom-right
- c07 — Centred clean composition, backlit steam, egg front-left
- c08 — Yolk dripping down the pull, chili-oil pools
- c09 — Wider full bowl on wood, brightest frame
- c10 — Black garlic oil swirl, huge foreground egg
- c11 — Steam maximum, egg halves open, yolk flooding the broth
- c12 — Ultimate hero: tall pull, egg front-centre, drip down the bowl

## Round 1 (6 matches)

- M1: c01 vs c04 → **c01**. c04's diagonal pull is dynamic and its egg drip
  is good, but its chopsticks float detached at the top of the frame, not
  holding the noodles, and its chashu is piled on the table outside the
  bowl — faults on criteria 4 and 3. c01 is complete and clean.
- M2: c02 vs c08 → **c02**. c08's yolk runs off the chopsticks in syrupy
  amber strands, which reads unnatural (criteria 2 and 4), and its egg is
  glossy but set, not molten. c02's pull and foreground egg are both huge,
  glossy and convincing.
- M3: c03 vs c05 → **c05**. c03's chili-oil swirl and steam are handsome,
  but no egg made it into the frame — the board's signature molten egg is
  missing — and the wider framing spends a third of the picture on empty
  table. c05 has the tallest pull in the field plus a yolk dripping over
  the rim. Criteria 1–3.
- M4: c06 vs c09 → **c06**. c09 is warm and its chashu fan is excellent,
  but it is the brightest frame, its background is not dark, and scallions
  are scattered across the table (criteria 3–4). c06's macro strands are
  glossier, fill the frame, and its jammy egg is bigger.
- M5: c10 vs c07 → **c10, narrow**. c07 is the cleanest composition in the
  field, but its pull is thinner and its chashu is pale and under-charred.
  c10's foreground egg is larger with yolk spilling to the rim, its char
  is deeper, and the black-garlic swirl gives the broth graphic contrast.
  Criteria 1–2.
- M6: c12 vs c11 → **c12, narrow**. c11's yolk flood is the single most
  molten moment in the field and its steam is the heaviest, but the splayed
  egg halves read slightly odd and the menma mass on the left competes with
  the pull (criteria 3–4). c12 is cleaner, with its signature thick drip
  down the outside of the bowl. c11 is the strongest eliminated candidate.

## Round 2 — semifinals (3 matches)

- S1: c01 vs c02 → **c01**. c02's egg is the biggest in the field, but in
  portrait crop the bowl all but disappears and the egg white reads as a
  thick disc. c01 keeps the whole bowl, a six-slice charred chashu fan and
  a yolk drip that runs all the way to the table. Criteria 3–4.
- S2: c05 vs c06 → **c05**. c06's chashu is pushed to the frame's edge and
  the dark nori mass muddies its base; c05's rolled chashu, complete bowl
  and rim drip read as one dish at any size. Criteria 3 and 5.
- S3: c12 vs c10 → **c12, narrow**. Both are elite and both pass the 300px
  test with room to spare. c12's egg sits front-centre with a thick drip
  outside the bowl and its chashu fan is the neater of the two; c10's
  swirl, striking as it is, covers half the broth surface. Criterion 3.

## Final — round-robin among c01, c05, c12

All three finalists (plus c10) were downscaled to 300px wide and re-checked:
every one still reads instantly — noodle column, orange yolk, chashu,
steam. Criterion 5 passes for all, so the final was decided on criteria 1–3.

- F1: c01 vs c05 → **c01, narrow**. c05 has the cleanest silhouette, but
  c01 shows more texture families at once: deeper char across six chashu
  slices, a longer yolk drip, heavier steam, a richer broth surface.
- F2: c01 vs c12 → **c01, narrow**. c12's yolk is bigger and dead centre;
  c01's frame is richer edge to edge — pull, chashu fan, egg, drip and
  steam with nothing wasted or competing. Criteria 2–3.
- F3: c12 vs c05 → **c12**. For runner-up: c12's front-centre yolk and
  external drip beat c05's plainer broth surface on criterion 1.

## Result

- **Winner: c01 — tall glossy noodle pull, whole bowl, charred chashu fan,
  molten ajitama dripping over the rim to the table, heavy steam, dark
  background** (candidates/c01-tall-pull-classic.png → entry.png, resized
  to 1024×1024 and stored as JPEG bytes, 254,410 bytes — PNG at that size
  was 1,319,984 bytes, over the 1 MiB gate; the gate accepts JPEG bytes
  under the entry.png name)
- **Runner-up: c12 — ultimate hero**
- Third: c05 — low-angle tallest pull

Why c01 wins the rubric overall: it is the most faithful and most complete
execution of the exact composition the live judge is rewarding at the top
of this leaderboard — one bowl, one tall glossy pull as the single dramatic
element, every texture family the rubric names visible at once (gloss on
the strands, melt in the dripping yolk, char/crunch on the chashu, steam
for heat) — in a square frame that survives the 300px test intact, and a
strictly stronger execution than the static incumbent it replaces.
